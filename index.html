About and sources

Independent university project, not affiliated with Mutti. Smart Industry course, University of Twente, challenge “From Workflow to Wisdom”. The case is the Montechiarugolo plant, rebuilt from public sources only. The Mutti logo and brand are not used.

The model moves one minute at a time. Each truck goes from the gate to core sampling, waits for the lab result, then unloads in a bay only when a tank has room for the whole load. The line draws from the fullest tank. The 3D scene replays each truck’s log: it is not connected to real plant data.

Free arrival and planned slots

With free arrival the trucks turn up when they like: 60% of them between 6:00 and 14:00, the rest across the day. This profile is our assumption. With planned slots each truck is given a time, spread evenly over 24 hours. Everything else stays the same, so the difference in the results comes from the arrival times alone.

Parameters

Sources

  • Linkiesta, August 2025 (Italian): 260 trucks and about 6,000 t a day on peak days.
  • Terra e Vita, about 2019 (Italian): eight trucks an hour unloaded at full capacity.
  • SMAU 2022, Mutti case (Italian): sampling, sorting and lab analysis.
  • ARPAE, permit DAMB/2021/2600 (Italian): authorised capacity of 3,200 t a day of finished product.
  • Mutti, 2022 press release: receiving open 24 hours a day during the harvest.
  • Public video of a plant visit, 2025 harvest: five tanks of about four truckloads each, and core sampling on every truck.

Limits

  • Three internal numbers are missing: core sampling time, waits at the gate and the share of rejected loads. The model shows mechanisms and orders of magnitude, not forecasts.
  • The public figure of 8 trucks an hour at full capacity does not fit 260 trucks a day (about 11 an hour needed). It is one of the questions for Mutti.
  • Times are fixed. In reality they vary from truck to truck.
  • The model does not track load quality (Brix, pH, colour) or blending in the tanks.
  • The 3D layout is schematic: positions and distances are not those of the plant.
